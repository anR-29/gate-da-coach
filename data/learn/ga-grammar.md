# Grammar, vocabulary and usage

> **Time:** ~30 min · **Pehle aana chahiye:** kuch nahi (bas basic English padhna aata ho) · **Day:** GA rotation me (har din ke page pe us din ka GA topic likha hota hai)

## Ye topic kyun?

GATE ke General Aptitude (GA) section me 15 marks hote hain, aur unme se 2–4 questions seedhe English par hote hain: blank bharo, sahi article chuno, analogy (A : B jaisa C : ?) pehchano, ya opposite word dhoondo. Ye mostly 1-mark MCQ hote hain, kabhi-kabhi 2-mark wala lamba sentence jisme do-teen blanks hote hain. Achhi baat: ye questions ratne se kam, **ek fixed method** se zyada solve hote hain. Ye lesson wahi method sikhata hai.

## Concept step-by-step

### 1. Pehle poora sentence, phir options

**Idea:** Blank ke aas-paas ka ek chhota tukda (clue) hi answer decide karta hai. Agar tum seedhe options dekhoge, toh jo word "achha sunta hai" usme fas jaoge.

**Rule:** Sentence ko end tak padho. Clue words ko mark karo: *although, but, yet, because, so, without, by, and, moreover, at once*.

**Example:** "The proposal was ______ rejected: members voted it down without even discussing it." Clue = *without even discussing it*. Matlab rejection jaldi aur bina formality hua. Word chahiye "turant, bina process ke" = **summarily**.

**Dhyan do:** *sumptuously* sunne me *summarily* jaisa hai, par matlab "shaan-o-shaukat se, mehenga" hai. Sound-alike word ka matlab apne words me bolo, tab match karo.

### 2. Articles: a / an sound se

**Idea:** *an* isliye exist karta hai taaki do vowel awaazein takraayein nahi ("a apple" bolna mushkil hai). Isliye rule awaaz par hai, letter par nahi.

**Rule:**
- Pehli **awaaz** vowel → *an*: an hour, an honest man, an heir.
- Letters jo apne naam se bole jaate hain aur naam vowel awaaz se shuru hota hai → *an*: an MBA ("em"), an SUV ("es"), an X-ray ("ex"), an FIR ("ef").
- Pehli awaaz "y" (u, eu) ya "w" (one) → *a*: a useful tool, a university, a European city, a one-day match.

**Example:** "It took us ______ hour to reach ______ European city that offered ______ MBA programme." → *an* (silent h), *a* ("yoo-ropean"), *an* ("em-bee-ay"). Answer: **an; a; an**.

**Dhyan do:** *a* MBA galat hai kyunki M likha consonant hai par bola "em" jaata hai — vowel awaaz.

### 3. for vs since, aur "by + future"

**Idea:** *for* batata hai **kitni der** (duration). *since* batata hai **kab se** (starting point).

**Rule:**
- for + duration: for six years, for two hours.
- since + point in time: since 2019, since Monday, since morning.
- Test: "How long?" ka jawab → *for*. "From when?" ka jawab → *since*.
- "By + future time" (by next March, by 2030) → **future perfect**: *will have + V3* (will have worked, will have finished). Kyunki us future point tak kaam **poora ho chuka hoga**.

**Example:** "By next March, Priya ______ at the institute ______ six years." → *By next March* = future point, so *will have worked*. *six years* = duration, so *for*. Answer: **will have worked; for**.

**Dhyan do:** "since six years" Indian English me bahut bola jaata hai, par GATE me ye galat hai.

### 4. Confusable words (milte-julte words)

**Idea:** Kuch words spelling me ek letter se alag hain par kaam bilkul alag karte hain.

**Rule:**
| Word | Kiske saath | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| assure | **person** | kisi insaan ko bharosa dilana (assure the inspectors) |
| ensure | **outcome** | pakka karna ki kuch ho (ensure power supply) |
| insure | **property / loss** | bima karwana (insure against flood) |
| affect | verb | asar daalna (it affected the result) |
| effect | noun | asar / nateeja (a strong effect) |

**Example:** "The judge's remarks had a strong ______ on the jury, but they did not ______ the sentence." Pehle blank ke aage *a strong* hai — adjective ke baad noun aata hai → *effect*. Doosre ke aage *did not* — iske baad verb aata hai → *affect*. Answer: **effect; affect**.

**Dhyan do:** Trick: **A**ffect = **A**ction (verb), **E**ffect = **E**nd result (noun). (Rare case: *to effect a change* = change laana, verb — par GATE me usually basic rule hi chalta hai.)

### 5. Subject–verb agreement

**Idea:** Verb ko apne **asli subject** se match karna hai, paas wale noun se nahi.

**Rule:**
- *The number of* + plural noun → **singular** verb (subject "number" hai, ek number): The number of applicants **has** risen.
- *A number of* + plural noun → **plural** verb (matlab "kai saare"): A number of students **were** absent.
- *Each of / every one of / either of* → **singular**: Each of the reports **has** errors.
- *Neither A nor B* / *Either A or B* → verb **B** ke saath (jo verb ke sabse paas hai): Neither the manager nor the clerks **were** present. Neither the clerks nor the manager **was** present.

**Example:** "Each of the reports ______ (have/has) a summary." Asli subject = *Each* (ek-ek report) → **has**.

**Dhyan do:** *reports* verb ke paas hai, isliye *have* sahi lagta hai. Pehle *of* ke pehle wala word dekho — wahi subject hai.

### 6. Analogies: relation ko sentence banao

**Idea:** A : B ka matlab hai "A aur B ke beech ek rishta". Wahi rishta, **same order** me, option me hona chahiye.

**Rule:** Ek chhota sentence banao jisme A pehle aaye: "A ____ B". Phir har option ko usi sentence me daalo.

**Example:** Cartographer : Map. Sentence: "A cartographer **makes** a map." Options: Composer : Symphony → "A composer makes a symphony" ✔. Symphony : Composer → "A symphony makes a composer" ✘ (order ulta). Answer: **Composer : Symphony**.

Doosra example: Drought : Famine → "Drought **causes** famine" (cause → effect). Negligence : Accident → "Negligence causes accident" ✔.

**Dhyan do:** Agar sentence bahut general hai ("A is related to B"), toh do-teen options fit ho jayenge. Sentence ko **specific** banao (makes, causes, is a type of, is used to measure).

### 7. Do-blank contrast sentences

**Idea:** Upar wali figure ka method: pehle polarity, phir words.

**Rule:**
1. Clue word dhoondo (*although* → ulta, *and* → same).
2. Jo blank ka hint sentence me hai, usse uski polarity fix karo.
3. Doosre blank ki polarity clue word se nikaalo.
4. Ab options me sirf woh pair rakho jiska pattern (+, −) match kare.

**Example:** "Although the reviewer praised the novel's ______ prose, she found its plot so ______ that she gave up halfway." *praised* → blank 1 = +. *gave up halfway* → blank 2 = −. Pattern (+, −). *lucid* (saaf, samajhne layak) = +, *tedious* (boring) = −. Answer: **lucid; tedious**.

**Dhyan do:** Agar option (+, +) hai jaise "lucid; gripping", toh woh *gave up halfway* ke saath fit hi nahi hota. Ek blank match karna kaafi nahi — dono check karo.

### 8. Phrasal verbs

**Idea:** Verb + chhota word (up, for, out, off) milkar naya matlab banate hain. Particle badla, matlab badla.

**Rule:**
| Phrasal verb | Matlab |
|---|---|
| cover up | chhupana (galti/scandal) |
| cover for | kisi ki jagah kaam karna, ya uska bachav karna |
| play up | badha-chadha kar dikhana (exaggerate) |
| play down | kam karke dikhana |
| make up | ghadna, invent karna (a story) / makeup |
| put off | taal dena (postpone) |
| call off | cancel karna |

**Example:** "After the data leak, the company tried to ______ the incident, but journalists soon uncovered the full story." *uncovered* ka ulta = chhupana → **cover up**.

**Dhyan do:** *cover for* bhi "cover" hai, par uska matlab hai kisi colleague ki jagah duty karna — chhupana nahi.

### 9. Vocabulary: synonym, antonym aur sound-alikes

**Idea:** Vocabulary questions me do trap hote hain: (a) milta-julta sound, (b) question OPPOSITE maang raha hai aur tum synonym chun lete ho.

**Rule:**
- Stem me **OPPOSITE / antonym** ho toh use underline karo.
- Har option ka matlab apne shabdon me ek line me bolo.
- Common pairs: *ephemeral* (kam samay tak rehne wala) vs *ethereal* (bahut naazuk, doosri duniya jaisa); *summarily* (turant, bina formality) vs *sumptuously* (shaan se, mehenga).

**Example:** Opposite of *ephemeral*? Ephemeral = short-lived. Opposite = long-lasting = **enduring**. Option *transient* bhi ho sakta hai — woh synonym hai, trap.

**Dhyan do:** *ethereal* sunne me *ephemeral* jaisa hai, par uska matlab "delicate, heavenly" hai — time se koi lena-dena nahi.

## Solved examples

**Example 1 (MCQ, 1 mark, easy):** Choose the correct articles: "He bought ______ umbrella and ______ uniform for ______ eight-year-old boy."
(A) an; a; an  (B) an; an; an  (C) a; a; an  (D) an; a; a

**Soch:** Articles → har word ki pehli awaaz bolo.

**Solution:**
1. *umbrella* → "um" → vowel awaaz → *an*.
2. *uniform* → "yoo-niform" → "y" awaaz → *a*.
3. *eight* → "ait" → vowel awaaz → *an*.
4. Answer: **(A) an; a; an**.

**Galti kahan hoti hai:** *uniform* ko "u" letter dekh kar *an* chun lena → option (B). Bola "yoo" jaata hai.

**Example 2 (MCQ, 1 mark, easy):** Select the pair with the same relationship as **Thermometer : Temperature**.
(A) Speed : Speedometer  (B) Barometer : Pressure  (C) Doctor : Fever  (D) Scale : Weight

**Soch:** Analogy → relation sentence, same order.

**Solution:**
1. Sentence: "A thermometer **measures** temperature."
2. (A) "Speed measures speedometer" — order ulta ✘.
3. (B) "A barometer measures pressure" ✔.
4. (C) Doctor fever ko measure nahi karta, treat karta hai ✘.
5. (D) Scale length naapta hai; weight weighing balance naapta hai ✘.
6. Answer: **(B)**.

**Galti kahan hoti hai:** (A) me dono words "sahi topic" ke hain, isliye jaldi me tick ho jaata hai — par order reverse hai.

**Example 3 (MSQ, 1 mark, medium):** Which of the following sentences are grammatically correct? (Select all that apply.)
(A) Neither the students nor the teacher was aware of the change.
(B) The number of complaints have doubled.
(C) A number of volunteers were waiting outside.
(D) Each of the machines need servicing.

**Soch:** Agreement → har sentence ka asli subject dhoondo.

**Solution:**
1. (A) Neither A nor B → verb B ke saath. B = *the teacher* (singular) → *was* ✔.
2. (B) *The number of* → singular → *has doubled* chahiye. *have* ✘.
3. (C) *A number of* = "kai" → plural → *were* ✔.
4. (D) *Each of* → singular → *needs* chahiye ✘.
5. Answer: **(A) and (C)**.

**Galti kahan hoti hai:** (A) me *students* plural dekh kar *were* sochna. Neither–nor me sirf doosra (paas wala) subject ginti me aata hai.

**Example 4 (NAT, 1 mark, medium):** In how many of the following five sentences is the word in bold used **correctly**?
1. The new rule will **affect** small shops.
2. The manager **ensured** the client that the delivery would be on time.
3. She has lived in Pune **since** 2018.
4. We waited **since** two hours.
5. The medicine had no **effect** on the fever.

**Soch:** Confusables + since/for. Har line alag check karo, phir gino.

**Solution:**
1. *affect* yahan verb hai (will affect) ✔.
2. Client ek **person** hai → *assured* chahiye. *ensured* ✘.
3. 2018 = starting point → *since* ✔.
4. two hours = duration → *for* chahiye ✘.
5. *no effect* — noun ✔.
6. Sahi = 1, 3, 5 → count = **3**.

**Galti kahan hoti hai:** Line 2 ko sahi maan lena (answer 4), kyunki *ensure* aur *assure* dono "pakka karna" lagte hain. Rule: person ko *assure*, outcome ko *ensure*.

**Example 5 (MCQ, 2 marks, hard):** "The minister tried to ______ the scale of the crisis in public, but the leaked report showed that the losses were far from ______."
(A) play up; serious  (B) play down; trivial  (C) cover for; negligible  (D) make up; severe

**Soch:** Do blanks + *but* (contrast) + phrasal verbs.

**Solution:**
1. *but the leaked report showed* → report ne minister ki baat galat sabit ki.
2. "losses were far from ______" — *far from X* = "bilkul X nahi". Report ne dikhaya ki loss bade the, toh losses "far from **small**" the. Blank 2 = "small" type word (*trivial*, *negligible*).
3. Minister public me crisis ko chhota dikha raha tha → **play down**.
4. Option (B): play down; trivial → "losses were far from trivial" = losses bade the ✔.
5. (C) *cover for* = kisi ki jagah kaam karna — "cover for the scale" ka matlab nahi banta ✘.
6. Answer: **(B)**.

**Galti kahan hoti hai:** *far from* ko miss karke blank 2 me *serious/severe* daalna. "far from serious" ka matlab ulta ho jaata hai (losses chhote the) — jo *but* ke saath contradiction hai.

## Confusions clear karo

**Q:** *an hour* sahi hai, toh *an house* kyun nahi?
**A:** *house* me "h" bola jaata hai ("haus"), toh pehli awaaz consonant hai → *a house*. *hour, honest, heir, honour* me "h" silent hai.

**Q:** *a university* aur *an umbrella* — dono "u" se shuru, alag article kyun?
**A:** *university* = "yoo-niversity" (y awaaz), *umbrella* = "um-brella" (vowel awaaz). Letter same, awaaz alag.

**Q:** "I am working here since 2019" galat hai?
**A:** *since 2019* sahi hai, par tense galat hai. Past se abhi tak chal raha kaam → present perfect continuous: "I **have been working** here since 2019."

**Q:** *The number of* aur *a number of* me itna farak kyun?
**A:** *The number of X* = "X ki ginti" — ginti ek hi number hai, singular. *A number of X* = "kai saare X" — X khud subject ban jaata hai, plural.

**Q:** Analogy me do options fit ho rahe hain, ab kya?
**A:** Tumhara relation sentence zyada general hai. Usse aur specific karo: "is related to" ki jagah "is the tool used to measure" ya "is the young one of" likho. Ab sirf ek option fit hoga.

**Q:** Vocabulary nahi aati toh kya karun?
**A:** Pehle polarity (+/−) se options kaato. Fir word ke parts dekho: *ephemeral* — "ephemera" = ek din wali cheez. Jo bache, usme sentence padh kar dekho kaunsa natural lagta hai. Saath me roz 5 naye words padhte raho.

## Ek page me

- Pehle poora sentence padho; clue word (although, but, without, by, and) answer decide karta hai.
- a / an: spelling nahi, **pehli awaaz** — an hour, an MBA, a useful, a European, a one-day.
- *for* + duration (for six years); *since* + starting point (since 2019).
- *By + future time* → future perfect: *will have + V3*.
- assure **person**, ensure **outcome**, insure **property/loss**.
- affect = verb (action), effect = noun (result).
- The number of → singular; a number of → plural; each of → singular; neither A nor B → verb B ke saath.
- Analogy: specific relation sentence banao, **same order** me test karo.
- Two blanks: pehle (+/−) polarity likho; although/but = ulta, and/moreover = same.
- Phrasal verbs: cover up (chhupana), cover for (jagah lena), play up / play down, make up (ghadna).
- Sound-alikes ka matlab apne shabdon me bolo: summarily ≠ sumptuously, ephemeral ≠ ethereal.
- OPPOSITE wale stem ko underline karo, synonym trap se bacho.
